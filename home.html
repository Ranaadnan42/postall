What PostAll does

Connect your accounts

Connect Facebook Pages and their linked Instagram accounts, your YouTube channel and TikTok with the official login of each platform.

One caption, one click

Upload a video or photo once, write one caption and tick the accounts. Press "Post to All" and PostAll publishes it everywhere you selected.

See the results

The History tab shows where each post went and whether it was published, with a link to the live post.

How we use YouTube

When you connect YouTube, PostAll asks Google for two permissions:

  • See your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) – used only to read your channel name and ID, so the app can show which channel is connected.
  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload) – used only to upload the video you choose, with your title and description, when you press "Post to All".

PostAll never uploads anything on its own, never edits or deletes your existing videos, and never shares your Google data with anyone. You can remove the connection in the app at any time. Read the Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. Create a free account with your email.
  2. Open Accounts and connect the platforms you use.
  3. Open Post, choose a file, write the caption and tick the accounts.
  4. Press Post to All.
