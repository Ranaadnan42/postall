Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

PostAll ("we", "the app") is a tool by Web Builder (Muhammad Adnan, Pakistan) that lets you publish photos and videos with one caption to the social media accounts you connect: Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, YouTube channels and TikTok, plus sharing to Snapchat. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (stored encrypted by our provider, Supabase).
  • Connected account data: when you connect a platform, we receive an access token and basic profile info (account or page name, ID and picture) so we can post on your behalf.
  • Content you post: the photos, videos and captions you choose to publish, and the result of each post (success or error).

How we use it

We use this data only to sign you in, show your connected accounts, publish the content you choose to the accounts you select, and show your posting history. We never post anything without your action, never sell your data, and never use it for advertising.

Google / YouTube data

PostAll uses YouTube API Services to upload videos you choose to your own channel. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. PostAll's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at Google security settings.

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok data

We request only the permissions needed to publish to the pages and accounts you manage. We do not read your private messages, friends or followers. You can remove PostAll at any time from your Facebook "Business Integrations" settings or TikTok "Manage app permissions".

Storage and security

Data is stored on Supabase servers with access restricted per user. Access tokens are kept on the server and are never shown in the app. Uploaded media is stored only as long as needed to publish it.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect any account in the Accounts tab, which deletes its token immediately. To delete your whole PostAll account and all data, email or WhatsApp us from your registered email; we delete it within 7 days.

Children

PostAll is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
